# Rabbitfish guided clips

Rabbitfish v1.3.0 adds Clip 05, **Inspect the specimen markings**, immediately
after Clip 04, **Tare the balance**. The specimen stays in its existing tray.
The five clip steps precede the retained orientation, anatomy and formative
knowledge check. Each playback clip is silent and no longer than ten seconds.

The new version contains metadata-stripped playback copies at
`resources/species-packs/rabbitfish/1.3.0/assets/clip-01.mp4` through
`clip-05.mp4`. Original supplied clips are organized locally under
`output/video-prompts/rabbitfish-72-20261030-v1/clips/`; that working folder is
excluded from the source download. The earlier v1.2.0 bundle is unchanged.

To preview locally, sign in as an administrator or authorized instructor, open
**Species content**, find Rabbitfish **v1.3.0**, select **View details**, then
**Preview draft**.
Continue through the preparation steps to Clip 05. The installer adds the new
version without replacing existing versions, assignments, accounts or results.

The clips retain their AI-generated-media declarations. Specimen identity,
continuity, pointer handling and laboratory procedure still require independent
faculty review. The new version starts in review, has no approval, and is not
automatically published, selected as current or assigned to students.

Deployment installs clip bundles through the dedicated clip seeder, without
replaying the broader demonstration course and enrollment setup. Existing
approved or published packages remain immutable.
